The research division of BeamNG.tech, a high-fidelity beam-node simulator for driving physics and modeling, highlighted three of my papers: "Automated Generation of Transformations to Mitigate Sensor Hardware Migration in ADS", "DeepManeuver: Adversarial Test Generation for Trajectory Manipulation of Autonomous Vehicles", and "PhysCov: Physical Test Coverage for Autonomous Vehicles".
